Privacy Policy for Navique Passage Planner


  Effective date: 20 May 2026


  Navique Passage Planner is provided by Tony Dagger. This Privacy Policy explains how

  the app handles information on iOS and Android.


  Navique Passage Planner is a marine passage-planning aid. It helps users enter vessel

  details, route points, waypoints, speeds, set and drift, weather limits, and related

  passage-planning information. Navigation calculations are planning aids only. Users

  must verify routes, depths, tides, weather, notices to mariners, and vessel position

  using official sources before departure.


  Information the App Uses


  The app may use the following information entered by the user:


  - Vessel name and vessel profile details

  - Draft, safety clearance, tide allowance, and minimum safe depth

  - Weather limit preferences

  - Compass deviation table values

  - Trip name and saved trip details

  - Point A, Point B, waypoint coordinates, speed, set, and drift

  - Notes or exported passage-planning information created by the user


  This information is used to calculate and display passage-planning outputs such as

  route distance, leg distance, accumulated distance, estimated leg time, accumulated

  time, true course, magnetic course, compass course to steer, and weather guidance.


  Location Information


  The app may request access to the device’s location when the user chooses to use the

  current location feature.


  Location information may be used to:


  - Set Point A, Point B, or waypoint coordinates

  - Display the current position on a map

  - Help calculate route and passage-planning information

  - Support weather and forecast checks for the planned route


  The app does not use location information for advertising or user tracking.


  Local Storage


  The app may store vessel profiles, saved trips, draft trips, compass deviation

  values, route points, weather limits, and settings locally on the device.


  This locally stored information remains on the user’s device unless the user chooses

  to export, share, email, print, back up, or otherwise send it outside the app.


  Exports and Sharing


  The app may allow users to export or share information such as:


  - Passage summaries

  - Trip maps

  - GPX files

  - KML files

  - Saved trip data

  - Email-ready or printable passage plans


  When the user chooses to export, email, print, or share information, that information

  may be handled by the selected email app, file-sharing service, cloud storage

  provider, printer service, or other third-party service chosen by the user. Those

  services are governed by their own privacy policies.


  Weather, Mapping, and External Services


  The app may use third-party services to provide maps, location display, magnetic

  variation lookup, weather forecast information, or marine forecast guidance.


  Depending on the platform and feature used, these services may include mapping

  providers, weather providers, government forecast sources, magnetic variation

  services, or other data providers. These services may receive technical information

  required to provide their service, such as map area, route area, coordinates, device

  network information, or request metadata.


  Where available, the app may prefer official forecast sources for Australian waters,

  such as Bureau of Meteorology forecast information. Forecast information is advisory

  only and must be checked against official sources before departure.


  No Advertising or Tracking


  Navique Passage Planner does not use user information for third-party advertising.


  Navique Passage Planner does not sell user data.


  Navique Passage Planner does not track users across other apps or websites.


  Analytics and Diagnostics


  The app may receive standard crash reports, diagnostic information, or app

  performance information through Apple, Google, or the relevant app store platform if

  the user has enabled such sharing at the operating-system level.


  This information is used only to help identify faults, improve stability, and

  maintain the app.


  Children’s Privacy


  Navique Passage Planner is not directed at children. The app is intended as a marine

  passage-planning aid for users who are responsible for planning and checking vessel

  passages.


  Data Security


  Reasonable care is taken to protect user information stored by the app. However, no

  method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Users should take

  care when exporting, emailing, sharing, or backing up trip and vessel information.


  Data Retention and Deletion


  User-entered information stored locally in the app remains on the device until the

  user deletes it, resets the app data, deletes saved trips or vessel profiles, or

  uninstalls the app.


  Exported files or shared information are controlled by the user and by the third-

  party service or destination selected by the user.


  User Choices


  Users can:


  - Decline location permission

  - Enter coordinates manually instead of using current location

  - Delete saved trips and vessel profiles

  - Avoid exporting or sharing files

  - Remove app data by deleting saved information or uninstalling the app


  Changes to this Privacy Policy


  This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be

  posted at the privacy policy URL used for the iOS App Store and Google Play listing.
